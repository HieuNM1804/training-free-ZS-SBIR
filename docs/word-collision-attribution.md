# Can the sketch change the order of two photos with the same leading noun?

Follow-up locked after reading the three word-axis audit tables. The first measurement compared each sketch's true and false photos inside its most likely noun. Multiplication by the sketch's positive confidence cannot change the ranking by the photo's confidence in that noun. Strong performance on this test alone can therefore come from one fixed gallery order for each noun.

In each split, group sketches and photos by their own leading word from the original Semantic Dot readout. Use the original labels retrospectively to form two distinct classes A and B under the same winning word. Require at least one sketch and photo in each class. Take all qualifying sketches. When a class-word group has more than 64 photos, retain 64 selected by the SHA-256 of dataset name, global photo ID and seed 42. This limit controls cost and does not enter any retrieval score. For every retained A/B photo pair, let u be the fraction of A sketches preferring photo A and v the corresponding fraction of B sketches preferring photo A. Give half credit to score ties. The probability both sketches prefer their own class is u(1-v), and the probability both prefer the other is (1-u)v. Report both, and their difference u-v. A fixed ordering yields zero expected difference for every pair, even if one of the photos is more typical. Swapping the sketches interchanges these two outcomes.
