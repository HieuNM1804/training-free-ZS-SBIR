For each sketch, keep every photo outside its own winning word in its original slot. Arbitrarily permute the photos inside the winning word's slots, including the possibility that a photo from below the official cutoff enters the prefix. Use relevance labels only to determine the mathematically best achievable per-query AP or precision under this restriction.

For each t, put the t positives at the earliest available ranks. Their count is fixed so these are the best ranks for that t. The largest AP across the counts is the exact attainable AP upper bound. The largest feasible t separately maximizes P@200. Replay the resulting actual photo permutations with the original KD-SBIR code.
