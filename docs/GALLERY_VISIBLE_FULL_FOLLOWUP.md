# Full study of what the target gallery can distinguish

On the balanced pilot the selected intervention strengthens observable differences by 0.8 and attenuates the remaining differences by 0.4. It improves Ext-2 mAP by 0.55 points while reducing Ext-1 mAP by 0.21. The gallery retained about 8–10% of the global variation and 43–48% of the regional variation in this measurement. A scalar surrogate matched to the two coefficients lost 1.06 mAP points relative to the proposed score on Ext-2. An independently tuned scalar control was considerably closer. These results motivate full-split selection and comparison against independently optimized controls.
