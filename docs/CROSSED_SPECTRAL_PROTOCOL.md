# Measuring whether a gallery mode is reproduced by other images and words

Our previous score changes the probability of matching the same word and improves on independent word draws by only 0.034–0.061 mAP points. Hard rejection is stronger. The current hypothesis concerns the spatial extent of a ranking error. A single incorrect gallery image or a single public concept can contribute to the ranking of an entire group after diffusion. The same word and the same photo must not by themselves be mistaken for replicated evidence about a category. A genuinely useful word that is the only description of a rare class is also a direct counterexample to this hypothesis.

Build the unlabeled gallery graph in RGB space or in the direct sum of centered CLIP image features and the expected public text direction. Both use the same ViT-B/32. The second term explicitly probes whether generic visual proximity captures a different nuisance from lexical similarity. A symmetric locally scaled kNN graph supplies the eigenvectors of its normalized adjacency. Let $\mu_w$ be the degree-weighted gallery prior of word $w$, $u_{jr}$ a graph coordinate, $d_j$ a vertex degree, $p_{jw}$ its public word posterior and $l_w$ the query's likelihood for that word. For a fixed query and graph coordinate, $X_{jw}=\sqrt{d_j}u_{jr}(p_{jw}-\mu_w)l_w$. Its sum $c_r$ is the ordinary query signal in that coordinate.

The energy contributed by pairs sharing neither a gallery image nor a lexical identity is exactly
$$
E_r = c_r^2-\sum_j(\sum_wX_{jw})^2-\sum_w(\sum_jX_{jw})^2+\sum_{jw}X_{jw}^2 .
$$
This is a deterministic sum of signed pair contributions. Distinct image identities do not imply independent CLIP errors, and distinct word identities do not certify distinct meanings. The statistic is not a calibrated probability. Use the positive part of the ratio $\sum_r E_r/\sum_r c_r^2$, capped at one, to decide how much evidence to retain in a spectral band for the current sketch. Both sums over a complete band are unchanged by an orthogonal change of basis within that band. The graph readout can separately assign eigenvalue-dependent weights. Four terms suffice to evaluate every photo/word pair exactly through precomputed tables. Inference uses no gradients, class names or other sketches.

Compare with the same graph, eigenvectors, rank, diffusion power and original-seed residual with a fixed gain, with photo exclusion only, with word exclusion only, and with a separately selected fixed spectral model. Compare full rankings with previously measured word diffusion and word-conditioned feedback using identical query ids. Estimate off-class witness mass only in a post-ranking diagnostic. For a retrieved photo, sample a word in proportion to its joint query–photo evidence and a second photo in proportion to its support for that word. Ground truth is used to measure how often this second photo has a different class, never to choose a donor. Compare with gallery-neighbor class purity.

Use 24 unseen sketches per class per Sketchy split for a coarse screen. Compare the top three fixed and top three crossed candidates using every Sketchy query, then freeze one of each on the mean official mAP across the two splits, with precision as tiebreaker. The registered grid includes graph choices RGB and RGB+text, 24 or 96 neighbors, 24/64/128 retained coordinates, groups of 8, eigenvalue powers 0/4 and residual 0/0.16. This uses the user's permission for exploratory tuning on Sketchy. The original KD metric function scores every final query and condition, including full 30-class TU-Berlin transfer. Query-side computation is independent for every sketch. Generic evidence pooling and the reliability statistic should not be credited with the same gain. A stronger fixed graph model defeats a claim that crossed replication is necessary for better accuracy.
