# Does the sketch itself predict which gallery decision is worth revisiting?

The previous acquisition system used the whole sketch to nominate rival groups, then predicted a useful crop from photographs. It beat a random action on balanced pilots but its locked full-Sketchy results were slightly below a single gallery-selected fixed view. The image content at a particular position may alter the value of zooming it, even among sketches with the same set of rivals.

Compute inexpensive previews during a single native CLIP visual pass. In the last block, keep the original CLS query, keys and values; restrict its keys to the CLS token and the cells of each allowed crop. Apply CLIP's ordinary output projection, residual, MLP and final projection. The full 7 by 7 mask must reproduce ordinary CLIP. These region embeddings share contextualized patches. They are observations at native resolution, not independent localized objects.

For each prospective action, represent the change between the preview and the whole image by its signed responses to public words in the appropriate domain prompts. Compare the sketch change to corresponding photo changes inside each group and each of two independent photo panels. For that group and panel, let photos with a similar preview predict which concepts a high-resolution observation might reveal. Normalize weights separately within each group to preserve the sketch's prior over rival groups. Choose the one actual high-resolution observation expected to reduce pair ordering errors in the other gallery panel. Then use the selected region's frozen lexical readout in the ordinary retrieval system.

The matched negative control breaks preview-to-zoom pairing within the same gallery group, panel and position while retaining both marginal sets. Further controls read a misplaced sketch preview, use the same bounded photo panel without conditioning, and choose a crop from the sketch's own coarse response. Measure whether conditioning improves prediction of the region's eventual concept profile and its retrieval utility. Effective sample size and the native full-view identity are logged. The pilot uses full Sketchy galleries, with one sketch processed at a time. The ranked result is reconstructed from every possible crop for evaluation only, after computing the selection. The three direct mechanisms are active sensing, lexical domain transfer and the pre-existing gallery operator.
