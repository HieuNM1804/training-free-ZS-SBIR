# Choosing among complete public-name rankings under uncertainty about the sketch's intended category

On Sketchy Ext-2 the first public name proposed by the whole sketch retrieves at 55.83 mAP@200. The best of its first 16 names, identified retrospectively with labels, retrieves at 83.48. Directly adding word mass to the locked regional retriever produced small and inconsistent improvements. Instead of making every proposal add evidence to every photo, the present hypothesis asks which *complete retrieval action* is best under multiple possible interpretations of the same sketch.

Discover visual groups from the unlabelled photo gallery. Each proposed word h supplies soft group memberships u(h,g) between zero and one, with the highest equal to one. Introduce a latent specificity threshold drawn uniformly for that word and shared by all gallery items. If the sketch intended h, relevant photos occupy all groups whose membership exceeds that threshold. This model describes uncertain population boundaries and correlated relevance. It does not supply true category labels.

Sort groups by decreasing membership for h. The probability that the first k groups are relevant is uₖ − uₖ₊₁. Let Rₖ be their total number of photographs. For a group g at position j, compute f(h,g) as the sum over k ≥ j of (uₖ − uₖ₊₁)/Rₖ. The expected normalized co-relevance of photos with groups g and b is C(g,b) = Σₕ p(h|q) min[f(h,g), f(h,b)]. This integrates both unknown name and unknown specificity exactly under the model. Every photo in a group shares the corresponding latent event. The sum of f over photographs is one for each h.

For any full ranking π, accumulate Lπ(g,b) by summing, over ranks r occupied by group g, the number of b items up to r divided by r. The scalar product of Lπ with C is the exact expected full-gallery average precision of π under these assumptions. The same utility is used on every dataset, including Sketchy Ext-2, whose official endpoint has a cutoff. The existing retriever and actions proposed by a single public word are options. Words are proposed independently by the current sketch. The incumbent ranking also supplies an optional intent hypothesis using its gallery-group response. All lookup tables for public-word actions can be computed before any sketch arrives.

First compare the base, the real AP of candidate actions, selection by expected AP, the first-order part of the same utility, and collapsing uncertainty into a single mean intent. The collapsed control preserves every diagonal element of C exactly, changing only relations between distinct groups. Compare with choosing the first word, weighting all proposed words, and choosing the alternative rated best by the incumbent alone. After selecting a candidate word, evaluate the available ranked mixtures with the incumbent for a conservative decision. Actual KD metrics and Oracle choices using ground truth are read after ranking. These distinguish poor candidate proposals, an incorrect relevance model, and a failure to select the useful ranking. No benchmark class list or other query enters the method.

Expected metric optimization for correlated retrieval outcomes predates this study. The proposed cross-domain mechanism is the common latent specificity of a public interpretation and the resulting exact, reusable group exposure calculation for a complete word action. The three direct sources are expected metric optimization, public-word domain transfer and the existing gallery operator.
