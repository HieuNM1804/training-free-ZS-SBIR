## Ranking protocol fixed before reading patch-verification outcomes

Generate the initial order with the full historical RGB diffusion operator and the paired sketch/photo vocabulary prompts. Use the first 128, 512 or 1,024 photos only for physical verification. For a verified photo, exponentiate its standardized patch score with a strength of 0.25, 0.75 or 1.5. Renormalize to preserve the original total evidence on the shortlisted photos. Compare using this correction on the final candidates with correcting their original lexical evidence before propagating the change across the complete gallery. The calibration requires one sketch only.

The matched competitor reads patches from both images in each of the two native reader frames and compares their foreground deviations from that image's mean patch value. Other controls keep each reader on its own image, use uniform patches, use a common gallery reader, omit the reference correction, or replace the candidate reader by one fixed unrelated gallery reader while retaining both original patch banks. Choose the top two configurations for the double exchange and the matched two-reader comparator using the same 24 sketches per class on each Sketchy split. Use both complete Sketchy splits for the final choice in each family. Then replay the selected interventions and controls through the original KD metric on all three splits. Neither the layer nor fusion parameters are selected on TU-Berlin.
