# Gallery alternatives as a visual reference game

The lexical seed and its historical RGB diffusion can strongly endorse a photograph of the wrong class. On the prior independent diagnostic, the expected off-class lexical witness mass was approximately 26–32% when the winning image was wrong. The spectral test did not show a consistent benefit from merely requiring different photos and words to support the same direction. Here the hypothesis is that a useful description must distinguish a group of gallery items from the particular alternative it competes with, and the decision must survive deletion of the most favorable donor.

Only unlabeled photos determine the competing groups. Oversegment normalized RGB features, or their equal-energy combination with public-word features, by spherical k-means. Ward linkage organizes the oversegmented prototypes into a binary tree. Every junction defines two alternatives, without providing class names or the number of benchmark classes. A source sketch supplies its CLIP likelihood over the public vocabulary. The standard word-to-photo diffusion remains the starting candidate score. This uses a single frozen CLIP ViT-B/32, no gradient, no contour, no sketch cohort.

A child group $C$ has predictive density $p_C(w)$, formed by the arithmetic or normalized geometric mean of its photo word profiles. A literal listener chooses between children using their population sizes and the expected query likelihood under each child. With arithmetic pooling, all literal choices on a path telescope exactly to the leaf's mean lexical evidence multiplied by its size. Simply placing the existing linear matcher in a hierarchy therefore supplies no new likelihood information.

Instead let a speaker favor words that distinguish $C$ from its sibling $D$. With equal intent priors the ordinary binary listener confidence is $p_C(w)/(p_C(w)+p_D(w))$. Define lower and upper bounds $L_C(w),U_C(w)$ on the word probability after deleting any one photo from $C$. For arithmetic pooling these use the group sum minus its maximum or minimum contribution, respectively. For geometric pooling, form the corresponding interval for the average log probability, then bound the normalizer by the sums of the coordinatewise upper and lower exponentials. The robust listener confidence $L_C/(L_C+U_D)$ is thus a guaranteed lower bound after independent single-image deletions in the two children. Exact leave-one-out examples are checked numerically. Singleton branches cannot certify a deletion and revert to the literal description.

The local pragmatic speaker is proportional to $p_C(w)$ times this confidence to a power. Normalize it over words before scoring the sketch. The product of pragmatic-to-literal branch probabilities gives a correction to the historical gallery score. A separate exponent controls the amount of within-leaf evidence retained. The original score is recovered at neutral pragmatic strength with full within-leaf evidence and arithmetic pooling. A matched zero-strength control measures ordinary group-level pooling. A stronger separately selected pooling-only control, sibling permutations, replacing siblings by the full gallery, and the ordinary listener without a deletion guarantee test which part is responsible for any improvement.

The two Sketchy splits serve as exploratory development data. Select 64 or 256 initial prototypes, two gallery geometries, arithmetic/geometric pooling, pragmatic strengths 2/8/24, within-leaf exponents .35/1, and update strengths .5/1. The top three robust and top two zero-strength configurations by average mAP on balanced pilots are compared on all Sketchy queries. Freeze winners before full 30-class TU-Berlin transfer. Evaluate each final condition on the original KD metric and inspect paired class-bootstrap intervals. Public benchmark labels only enter the evaluation.

Andreas and Klein implement a pragmatic listener for reference in context. Fan et al. measure how human sketches change with distractors, which motivates testing this form of selectivity in SBIR. Here the target alternatives, stable lexical messages and likelihood correction are inferred solely from unlabeled photos and the frozen model. The foundation is the existing public-word bridge and RGB diffusion.
