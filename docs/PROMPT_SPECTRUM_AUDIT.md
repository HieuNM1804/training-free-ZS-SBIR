# What changed when the signed head intervention reordered photographs?

This post-hoc experiment uses the original frozen head intervention from commit ea8c50f. There are four predefined conditions on all three benchmarks. For each sketch let u be the original logits over the 20,931 public names and v the edited logits. The rank-only condition keeps the complete sorted list of original scores (and hence its softmax entropy) but assigns those scores to words in their edited order. The spectrum-only condition keeps the ranking of the original words and assigns the edited sorted scores to them. The full and base conditions are the other two corners of this comparison. Exact scatter reconstruction and agreement with the previously archived terminal points are checked.

The regional branch remains the same. The original metric receives the final ranked permutations for each query. Report all four outcomes, paired class intervals, literal-name movements among retrieval errors, class-specific effects, and a fixed and a broken top-one example nearest to the median observed AP change of each group. The query and photo paths in these examples are descriptive. Labels enter after the ranking. These four conditions give a mechanistic sensitivity analysis of the frozen setting. All three datasets' outcomes were already known before specifying the analysis. [Full signed-head experiment](results/paired-prompt-head-intervention/FULL_VI.md)
