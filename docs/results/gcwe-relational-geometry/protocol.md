# Do two correspondences verify structure, or do centrally placed patches simply match other central patches?

Draw two independent sketch–photo correspondences (A,B) and (A',B') from the frozen patch coupling C. Original GCWE scores the correlation of X=d(A,A') and Y=d(B,B'). Define u(a)=E[X|A=a]−E[X] and v(b) analogously. Form X2=X−E[X]−u(A)−u(A') and Y2 analogously. Expanding shows Cov(X,Y)=2 E_C[u(A)v(B)] + E[X2 Y2]. Mixed terms vanish by conditioning and independence of the two draws. Centrality here means expected distance under the candidate-specific patch marginal, rather than distance to a fixed image center.

The first term can support a match when patches of similar eccentricity correspond. The second is a weighted, double-centered distance covariance measuring residual pairwise structure. The algorithm tests using just the second contribution, with the original denominator, for GCWE donor ranking. Also evaluate normalization by residual variances, the first term alone, and the same second term after shuffling physical gallery patch coordinates. Apply each measurement to the real sketch and the same blank canvas. Check recomposition of both terms for every candidate and every real and blank observation. The decomposition occurs before donor ranking. It does not imply additivity of mAP.

Distance-covariance pooling has precedent in visual recognition; spatial verification before query expansion has precedent in retrieval. The research question concerns which part of an exact donor verification statistic supports *word enrichment* across domains in frozen CLIP. [Xie et al., CVPR 2022](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/papers/Xie_Joint_Distribution_Matters_Deep_Brownian_Distance_Covariance_for_Few-Shot_Classification_CVPR_2022_paper.pdf) · [Chum et al., ICCV 2007](https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~vgg/publications/2007/Chum07b/)

Use 1,536 queries at seed-42 permutation positions 6,912–8,447 in each Sketchy split and score every gallery photo. Only the verification statistic changes. For promotion require a gain in equal-split mean mAP over intact GCWE, and gains over both eccentricity-only and wrong-coordinates on each split. A true-versus-shuffled gap can support the geometry interpretation even if the original sum is a better estimator. Precision, class-level uncertainty, the no-GCWE reference, and the exact original replay accompany every claim. No TU tuning.
