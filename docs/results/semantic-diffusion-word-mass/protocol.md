# Restore semantic alternatives in the query's gallery distribution

In the complete three-module baseline, turning on global diffusion with regional diffusion already on gave 3.58, 1.46 and 3.02 mAP points on Ext-1, Ext-2 and TU. It also removed the final relevant photo in the top 200 for 785, 1,025 and 119 queries. This test addresses a specific hypothesis: diffusion reallocates too much probability between distinct meanings of a sketch, even when its ranking within a meaning remains useful.

For a single query normalize direct word agreement across gallery photos into a_i and its diffused counterpart into d_i. B_iw is photo i's original probability of word w. Let A_w=sum_i a_i B_iw and D_w=sum_i d_i B_iw. Under the diffused joint J(i,w)=d_i B_iw keep J(i|w), but replace its marginal D_w by the directly observed A_w. The new gallery mass is h_i=d_i sum_w B_iw A_w/D_w. This solves the forward KL projection min_K KL(K||J) subject to K's *word* marginal being A, by the KL chain rule. Re-encoding h with B need not have marginal A. This is a query-dependent distribution correction across photos after scoring, distinct from standardizing the distribution of graph endpoints within each candidate before scoring.

Writing h and d as outputs of the same posterior word-to-photo Markov kernel proves TV(h,d) <= TV(A,D) <= TV(a,d). The code checks both bounds on every query. A matched linear interpolation between a and d moves exactly the same total probability as h for each sketch. An additional sham permutes the word ratios among frequency-adjacent words. Labels do not enter any calculation. Prior-probability shift correction motivates the distinction between changing a prior and keeping a conditional fixed. Whether the conditional is useful for SBIR requires direct testing.
