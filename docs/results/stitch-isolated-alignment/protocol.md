# Test STiTch-style alignment by itself in SBIR

For every independent sketch, select its five highest source-prompt nouns from the approved 20,931-word public dictionary. Encode the same five nouns with target photo prompts. Every gallery candidate has ten unit CLIP embeddings: the unaltered benchmark view and nine path-seeded random crops with horizontal flips. Crop area is 0.5–1 of the benchmark view and aspect ratio 3:4 to 4:3, rendered on Colab GPU. The matrix C contains all 50 noun–view cosines. No old gallery graph or baseline is consulted, and there is no shortlist reranking. The paper's text modification and sampled MLLM captions do not exist in the single-sketch task. The experiment transfers only the alignment mechanism. A name hypothesis here can be an alternative to another name, while CIR captions generally intend to describe the same edited scene.

The prespecified grid gives all candidates identical inputs. Change the readout alone. Compare a single noun and original view, five nouns and original view, one noun and ten views, the entire matrix mean, both directional expected similarities and the symmetric STiTch-style readout. The implementation reproduces the repository's conditional similarity at tau 0.07. Separate ablations measure the bidirectional score on the additive matrix of noun and view marginals, and the overall mean plus only the transport excess above this additive null. A positive transport effect must beat the same-input matrix mean. When the additive null matches it, specific noun–view alignment has not demonstrated additional value.

An exploratory SBIR hypothesis requires the same noun to explain a candidate's different views. Its readout is a log-sum-exp over nouns after averaging views. The competing model averages the per-view log-sum-exp over nouns, which allows each view a different noun. Their difference equals tau times the mean reverse KL from the consensus noun posterior to the individual view posteriors. This identity is numerically checked. The shared-noun score depends only on the mean gallery feature; gains over weaker baselines do not establish new spatial grounding. No parameters or receiving dataset are chosen from TU. Evaluate the original full galleries with the KD-SBIR metrics on Colab ViT-B/32.
