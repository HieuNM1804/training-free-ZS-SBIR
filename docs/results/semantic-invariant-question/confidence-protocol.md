# Does the cost of reversing a sketch decision predict when it is trustworthy?

This is a secondary test of the *meaning of the certificate*. It was specified while the expensive pair optimization was running and before inspecting the pilot's decision outcomes. Even two sketches that vote equally strongly under one optimal question can require very different sacrifices of photo discrimination to reverse their votes. If certainty comes from words of negligible probability under either photograph, the certificate can be nearly zero despite a large absolute vote.

Use three fixed confidence readings from the same problem: the lower bound on photo accuracy lost to reversal, the absolute sketch vote under one optimizing question, and the fitted discrimination of the photo pair alone. Measure each reading's ability to pick the more trustworthy of two decisions. Give a tie half credit, without fitting a threshold, selecting a coverage, or changing any original decision. Analyze the invariant and pooled rules separately.

* At category boundaries, hold the query fixed. Use only queries for which one of its two certified binary decisions is correct and the other wrong. Ask whether higher confidence identifies the correct decision. Bootstrap the retained classes with equal weight and disclose how many queries and classes remain.
* For redraws, hold the two rival photographs fixed. A source's decision is stable when the two independently processed drawings agree. When exactly one of the two sources has a stable decision, ask whether the confidence of its first drawing exceeds the confidence of the other source's first drawing. Repeat with the other drawing as the observer, then average the two comparisons inside each rival pair. This compares predictions obtained from single sketches. Source pairing and redraw identity enter the assessment only. Exclude pairs with a tied decision, retain the exclusion counts. Photo-only confidence must tie under this matched design. Report uncertainty over independent comparison IDs and distinguish same-category from different-category rivals.

Require advantages over the single-question vote on both development splits before claiming that the reversal cost contributes a reliability signal. Agreement across drawings and accuracy against classes are distinct endpoints. This analysis does not report benchmark mAP or invoke a fallback ranker.
