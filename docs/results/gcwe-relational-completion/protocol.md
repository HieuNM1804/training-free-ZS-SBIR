# Does a reusable relation between two observed parts imply the meaning of a missing part?

A single whole-image word failed to mediate local observations in the preceding study. A different hypothesis is that the *meaning of a specific missing part* can connect two other observations even when they come from different photos. Part here means a disjoint region read by CLIP. No part annotations are available.

For each pair of visible sketch corners A,B and distinct held-out corner C, form one unlabeled donor panel in the opposite gallery fold. Take the union of the 32 visually closest photos to each observed crop, with equal weight for each unique photo. The withheld corner, the whole sketch, its reference ID and other drawings cannot enter the panel. Encode every native 96×96 corner independently in both domains. Local categorical observations use the same 64 public words in the split's regional module. Whole-photo semantic descriptions use all 20,931 public words. The four corners have one latent frame, integrated over the eight symmetries of the square. Include all 12 choices of C and unordered A,B. No fit, labels or gradients enter inference.

Let L_A(d,g) be the overlap probability between the observed A and the donor readout at its putative location in photo d in frame g. Define L_B similarly. Let h(d,g,c) be the donor's local probability of hidden word c at C. Define r_dc = E_g h, u_dc = E_g[h L_A] and v_dc = E_g[h L_B]. Under a generative tree with hidden c as the shared cause, the two anchors draw their donor and frame separately from the same conditional law. Its unnormalized posterior is proportional to (Σ_d u_dc)(Σ_d v_dc)/(Σ_d r_dc). This tree has the same one-anchor marginals as the literal joint model. Under the literal model the numerator for c is proportional to Σ_d E_g[h L_A L_B]. Their difference before normalization is the conditional covariance of the two anchor evidences times the prior of c. Thus merely constructing a triple from intact photo observations would recover the literal baseline; the intervention changes a specific conditional independence assumption.

The primary model explicitly requires two distinct photo identities given c. Conditional on distinct draws, its posterior is proportional to

    (Σ r) [(Σ u)(Σ v) − Σ uv] / [(Σ r)² − Σ r²] .

Each sum runs over unique donors for c. Use double precision and the valid limiting independent-tree score in a numerically degenerate denominator; count such cells. Report ordinary tree, same-donor conditional on c with independently chosen frames, literal donor and frame, prior, and the distinct tree with all 24 position permutations. The last replaces the adjacency/diagonal relation by uniformly distinct locations while retaining every photo and local word distribution. Validate all formulas against explicit finite sums and verify that one uninformative observation reduces the ordinary tree to the literal single-anchor posterior. The distinct condition can change its single-anchor marginals; its ordinary-tree control detects this effect.

Measure held-out expected log probability of C using its independently encoded sketch crop. Evaluate on 768 queries per Sketchy split using a seed-42 permutation from position 6,912, skipping every query ID in either preceding geometry-to-word pilot in either split. Use only the forecast for which the reference photo is outside the donor fold for the mechanistic endpoint. Report separately the ordinary panels and a controlled stress test that removes the upper quartile of donor photos by their joint A,B likelihood, computed without C. The stress test investigates whether genuine gains appear only when the strongest exemplar explanations are unavailable; it cannot replace the ordinary result.

For the semantic readout, transport the inferred c to scene words via p(d|c) and each donor's native whole-photo word distribution. Also read the literal model directly through its posterior over donors and through the same c-to-word decoder. Evaluate the change in proper log score on the rest of the drawings of the same photo and on the actual reference photo using the held-out fold. For category retrieval score all gallery photos, applying the existing gallery graph and the unchanged KD evaluator. Compare with the native two-view semantic module. Priors, folds and cohort stay fixed in all arms.

Primary prediction: in ordinary panels the distinct role-mediated model predicts C better than both the literal exemplar and the all-permutation control on both splits. An added gain over the ordinary tree is needed to support forced cross-photo composition. An improvement on the observed class metric alone would not support the generative claim. These are exploratory tests. Only a mechanism that survives the matched controls warrants a locked TU transfer.

Pairwise factorization and part-based object models are established. The contribution under test is inferring the semantics of a withheld part through two distinct cross-domain witnesses and measuring the conditional dependence discarded by that abstraction.
