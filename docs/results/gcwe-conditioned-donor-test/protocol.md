# Does geometric feedback identify semantic evidence beyond CLIP's global visual rank?

The existing system scores a photo with 0.65 times global log word agreement after RGB diffusion, 0.35 times the candidate-conditioned regional log score, and the difference in global log agreement induced by GCWE with coefficient 0.65. All three modules share a public vocabulary. Masking the global score leaves that vocabulary available to both remaining modules. Turning off diffusion leaves direct global agreement in the score.

## Paired donor test

The original GCWE multiplies the query probability of a word by O/U, where O is the geometry-weighted word mass of the nominees and U is their uniform word mass. Each count includes the original one-photo prior. This update may reward words simply because they occur toward the top of CLIP's global cosine list.

Within each held-out-gallery protocol, order the eligible donors by their original CLIP cosine rank. Pair consecutive donors, fixing an odd singleton. For each pair, replace both geometric weights by their average. This gives the exact expected word mass M under independent within-pair exchanges. It preserves total weight in each pair and preserves the original photo word profiles. The proposed GCWE uses O/M. A between-pair control uses M/U. For each word, log(O/U) equals log(O/M) plus log(M/U), before query normalization. This identity applies to word evidence; mAP is nonlinear. Singleton blocks give an identity update, and a single block recovers the baseline up to the rank-weight mass normalization. Adjacency in visual rank is an approximate control for visual similarity. It does not imply that two donors are exchangeable at the semantic level.

Prior work demonstrated spatial verification for query expansion and local permutation for conditional dependence testing. Here the expected local-null word count is used inside a frozen cross-domain retrieval score. This is a proposed transfer and needs evidence against both a random-pair control of equal size and the existing update. [Chum et al., ICCV 2007](https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~vgg/publications/2007/Chum07b/) · [Runge, AISTATS 2018](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v84/runge18a.html)

## Other two modules

Candidate-conditioned regional matching produces a distribution over the query windows for each candidate. The shared-attention control averages these distributions across candidates, then measures the same candidate-specific regional evidence. Every window, pose, candidate and coefficient stays fixed. This isolates candidate-dependent selection within a given query. SCAN already established pair-conditioned attention in retrieval. Additional novelty must come from a specific, experimentally verified mechanism for conditioning a frozen model. [Lee et al., ECCV 2018](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_ECCV_2018/html/Kuang-Huei_Lee_Stacked_Cross_Attention_for_Image-Text_Matching_ECCV_2018_paper.html)

Cross-domain semantic retrieval computes a dot product of domain-prompted word probabilities and smooths it over the gallery. We measure the direct word score, its smoothed counterpart and the same smoothed score with word identities exchanged between adjacent gallery-frequency ranks. A two-view image cosine uses the same gallery. The isolated global-term ablation zeros its additive contribution in the complete estimator. FREEDOM is a direct training-free precedent for expressing content through public words to retrieve across domains. Merely doing so cannot establish originality. [Efthymiadis et al., WACV 2025](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2025/papers/Efthymiadis_Composed_Image_Retrieval_for_Training-Free_Domain_Conversion_WACV_2025_paper.pdf)

## Evaluation fixed before pilot results

Use the original evaluator, seed 42, 768 uniformly sampled queries per Sketchy split and every gallery photo. The same rows score every comparison. Ranked candidates, gallery folds, regional weights and diffusion settings match the archived baseline. The matched update should improve the equal-weight mean mAP over original GCWE and beat random pairing on both splits to justify a full rerun as the primary. If the between-pair control explains the gain and the within-pair update does not, interpret this as evidence against suppressing coarse donor-rank structure. TU stays outside this selection.
