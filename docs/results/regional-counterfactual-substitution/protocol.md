# Can photo parts substitute for sketch parts in a joint semantic observation?

Jointly encoding the two sketch strips recovered 17.57 / 16.96 mAP points versus a raw additive extrapolation from the same 42 patches. Previous methods tried to transfer a precomputed regional response from one depiction to the other. Instead test whether one intact part of the candidate can serve as context for an observed sketch part, directly in the frozen ViT. This is a counterfactual inference rule for Candidate-Conditioned Regional Matching. Use native patch embeddings with original positions, the same CLS and no gradient. Construct all four assignments of source (sketch or photo) to two disjoint strips. Each composite is encoded jointly through CLIP. Compare left/right and top/bottom cases with the identical center gap. Use bare public text CLIP prompts, identical for all four images.

Let A and B be independent fair supplier bits, W a public word drawn from the CLIP output for that composite. Form P(a,b,w)=P(w|a,b)/4. A change caused by one supplier may persist for all choices of the other, and the naming probabilities for the four composites can differ substantially. Remove *all* effects reproducible from the three pairwise marginals P(A,B), P(A,W), P(B,W): construct their maximum-entropy completion P₂. It has constant 2×2 supplier odds ratio across words. For each word its two univariate supplier marginals and one shared log odds specify a unique binary table; solve the single global constraint that the (1,1) cell has mass 1/4. Verify marginal recovery, common odds up to round-off for supported cells, KL(P||P₂)=H(P₂)−H(P), and zero interaction for arbitrary additive word logits plus a joint condition offset. This explicitly removes simple script/style differences that are additive in word logits. Nonlinear style effects can remain. The working hypothesis is substitutability: for the right category, which depiction supplied one side should change the meaning of the other side less. Primary discrepancy is the mean conditional KL between P(A,B|w) and P₂(A,B|w), where words are weighted by the intact query's independent public readout. It tests whether substitutions disrupt the meanings plausible for the actual sketch. Also report the ordinary third-order information under the pooled word prior, total information, its lower-order part and the direct Jensen–Shannon divergence of the two unmixed views. All scores are averaged over the two axes. Lower means greater substitutability. When the candidate equals the sketch the four readings coincide and the discrepancy vanishes. Swapping the two supplier names preserves the pooled information exactly. The candidate never reads another sketch. Synthetic and native identity replay checks precede analysis. Fixed protocol on the previously diagnosed class boundary cohort. Require primary balanced category accuracy above chance and above the native unmixed or lower-order disagreement on both splits, with paired class intervals. Report all tested pairs, tie convention, newly correct and newly wrong decisions. A method that merely detects style disruption in hybrids or repeats simple single-image disagreement does not establish conditional semantic information. This probe has no score fusion or tuned coefficient.
