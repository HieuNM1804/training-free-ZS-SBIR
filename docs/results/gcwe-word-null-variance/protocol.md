# Which geometry-to-word enrichment exceeds the noise produced by random donor identity?

GCWE estimates a separate relative lift for each public word from approximately one hundred eligible nominees. It can report the same relative change for a word supported by many photos and for a word whose mass is concentrated on a single donor. Removing fold sampling by exact expected counts in an earlier pilot barely changed aggregate AP. That test did not measure uncertainty in the association between geometric donor weights and a particular word.

Condition on the n eligible donors and their geometric weights a and word probabilities b_w. Under random exchange of donor identities the variance of sum_i a_i b_iw is the product of the centered sums of squares of a and b_w divided by n−1. The variance of the lift follows by dividing by the squared reference count including its smoothing prior. Verify this identity by enumerating the permutations on a small example. The null is a diagnostic exchangeability model; distractor photos in a real gallery can be correlated.

Let d_w be the observed lift minus one and v_w its null variance. Estimate a common nonnegative signal second moment T from sum_w p(w|q)(d_w²−v_w). Under a centered normal random-effect approximation, shrink each lift deviation by T/(T+v_w). Exactness applies to the permutation variance, not to the observational noise or normal approximation. Controls apply one weight per query and fold matched to the same query-weighted RMS lift change, or misassign precisions between frequency-adjacent words. Geometry scores, candidate words and all original parameters are fixed. This adapts variance-based shrinkage to GCWE's finite donor proposal. [Muandet et al., ICML 2014](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v32/muandet14.html) · [Chum et al., ICCV 2007](https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~vgg/publications/2007/Chum07b/)

Primary evaluation on 1,536 queries per Sketchy split, seed 42, positions 5,376 through 6,911. Require improved equal-split mAP over original GCWE and an advantage over both precision controls on each split before full evaluation and locked TU transfer. Include the no-GCWE control to detect gains from merely switching off a harmful update.
