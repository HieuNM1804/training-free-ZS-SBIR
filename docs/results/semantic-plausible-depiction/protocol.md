# Is the advantage of inferring a partial depiction specific to the candidate, once its content is conserved?

The unanchored partial-view model predicted another word on independent drawings more accurately, but often accommodated a wrong-class photograph even better. A latent channel for how the content of **a given photo** will be depicted should have a testable invariance: before any drawing observation, averaging over its possible signals must recover the intact photo's semantic distribution. The uniform mixture of 16 CLIP partial views generally changes that distribution as well as introducing conditional word dependence. Separate these two mechanisms.

Let J₀(m,w)=b_m(w)/16 be the unanchored joint distribution and r(m|w)=J₀(m,w)/ΣₖJ₀(k,w). Let a(w) be the intact photo word distribution. Impose P(W)=a and compute J(m,w)=a(w)r(m|w). This is the unique finite KL projection minimizing KL(J‖J₀) subject to this single marginal, by the conditional KL chain rule. Define ρ(m)=Σ_w J(m,w) and h_m(w)=J(m,w)/ρ(m). Then Σ_m ρ(m)h_m(w)=a(w) exactly and P(m|u)=r(m|u) exactly. Conditional dependence of two draws with replacement has Bayes factor Σ_m r(m|u)r(m|v)/ρ(m) against independent draws from the full photo. It has expectation one over v drawn from a for each u. Thus all alternatives agree on evidence from **a single word**, including the query word that selected the partial view. Only a shared semantic explanation can distinguish them. The distinct-word forecast uses the exact conditional with h_m(v)/(1−h_m(u)) in each state.

Compare (i) shared view, (ii) a fresh independent view drawn from ρ, (iii) independent word draws from a, (iv) keeping only the inferred number of visible regions and drawing a new view from ρ conditional on that size, and (v) a false correspondence model. For (v) permute the names of the partial views independently for every word **within subset-size groups**, recompute ρ and each view distribution with the intact content a fixed, and repeat exactly the same inference. This preserves the entire sorted distribution of view probabilities and probability of each size for every word but severs which words nominate the same parts. Also test one common permutation of all views as an exact invariance and the KL projection identity. No learned weights, gradient, vocabulary thresholds or new data.

First reuse the discovery source pairs and all 128 query-word observations. Report both the conditional forecast and the two-observation joint score including log a(u); the latter is a valid per-sketch score when the second observation also comes from the current sketch. Independent redraw targets are a diagnostic. Since the first marginal is fixed, the joint and conditional *method effects* must be exactly equal. Measure intact-to-mixture marginal drift in KL. Require better true-source forecast and better true-minus-foil separation than **both** the same-size and false-correspondence controls on both splits and report pair-bootstrap uncertainty. A marginal-preserving transformation can still compensate an improbable first word by overfitting a wrong photo. Prior art distinguishes the general expectation-of-posteriors constraint and prior correction from this test of which content is jointly depictable within a specific image.
