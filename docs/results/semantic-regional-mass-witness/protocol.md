# How much direct word mass should this query retain?

Full lexical marginal restoration recovered many queries but cost 1.18 and 0.12 mAP on the two Sketchy pilots. A separate confirmation showed that the regional scale-only readout is better at standalone ranking than fully candidate-dependent positions, while the latter helps once global semantics are included. Use the scale-only regional readout as a second measurement of the plausible alternatives and keep the full original regional score in the final ranking.

Turn scale-only regional scores into a probability distribution over the gallery. Reading its expected photo-word histogram gives C. Direct global retrieval and diffusion give A and D in the same word coordinates. Choose beta in [0,1] that minimizes squared distance from D+beta(A−D) to C, with the analytic solution given by the clipped inner product (C−D)·(A−D) divided by ||A−D||². If A=D, use zero. This is also the minimum expected multiclass Brier loss when the word target has distribution C. C is a model prediction, not ground truth, and the two channels share a vocabulary and gallery. Apply this fraction to the preceding KL projection of gallery mass. The construction adjusts the semantic module using an existing frozen regional observation.

Controls hold the regional score fixed in the final sum. They use the native diffused score, fixed restoration fractions of 1/4, 1/2, 3/4 and 1, the adaptive fraction on a direct interpolation with equal total variation, and a fraction computed after shuffling regional mass across gallery photos. Evaluate with the original 0.65 / 0.35 weights, without GCWE to isolate the mechanism. Lock the pilot on seed-42 positions 5,376 through 8,447 per Sketchy split, disjoint from the lexical projection pilot within each split. Before full confirmation require gains in top-200 coverage on both splits, no loss of equal-split mean mAP versus native, and higher equal-split mAP than the best fixed fraction, shuffled witness and equal-motion direct control. No TU tuning.
