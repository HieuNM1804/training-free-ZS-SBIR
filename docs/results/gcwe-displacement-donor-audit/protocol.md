Reuse the exact nominees, native word posteriors, split folds, blank subtraction, rank densities and the 1,536 confirmation queries per Sketchy split. Join the official per-query AP values stored by that independent run. The candidates and word updates receive no benchmark labels. Evaluate the weights and emitted word distributions afterward using the benchmark labels in two ways.

1. Under the exact winning-donor law, compute the probability the selected donor belongs to the sketch's class. Uniform donors are the selection baseline.
2. In the opposite, evaluation fold, compute the difference between the mean posterior of relevant and irrelevant photos. Its inner product with the change in the sketch's word posterior equals the change in mean relevant-minus-irrelevant direct word overlap. This is measured before diffusion. Average the two readouts by target-fold size.

An increase in donor purity is evidence that the verifier selects better category representatives. A larger held-out margin is evidence that the semantic update transfers in the intended direction.
