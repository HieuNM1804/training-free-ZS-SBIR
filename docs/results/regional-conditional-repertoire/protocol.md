# Candidate-conditioned prediction from a set of possible semantic changes

The preceding matched transfer assumed that two locations induce the very same semantic change in both depictions. The directional audit found much stronger within-sketch-domain agreement. A different hypothesis is that a photograph defines a family of possible changes, and the sketch indicates which member was realized. For each candidate use the six nonadditive effects of part pairs in that photo as a linear subspace in the public word log-odds. A permutation or invertible reparameterization of its basis preserves the model; transferring a named part interaction at a fixed scale does not. This is an inference rule inside Candidate-Conditioned Regional Matching. The subspace reconstruction principle has prior art in few-shot recognition. The quantity being reconstructed here is a counterfactual response to a joint intervention across domains. [FRN, CVPR 2021](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2021/papers/Wertheimer_Few-Shot_Classification_With_Feature_Map_Reconstruction_Networks_CVPR_2021_paper.pdf) · [Feature interactions, ICML 2023](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/li23at.html)

For one observed sketch pair form the raw-feature additive base from its lower-order observations. Reveal the centered log-odds departure of its joint CLIP reading on **only one half** of the 20,931 public words. This is a held-word forecast, not a forecast of an entirely unobserved image. Estimate per-query/candidate coordinates in the candidate's subspace by exact least squares on the revealed coordinates, then forecast the joint reading of the other half. Center each observed vector by its arithmetic mean over revealed words to remove the arbitrary log-normalization constant. Held-out forecasts are scored by proper log score against the frozen CLIP distribution conditional on the withheld word set. A second drawing from the same source is an independent evaluation target. Exchange both halves and average. Vocabulary halves are separated along a text-only semantic axis: choose two public word embeddings by seed 42, order the vocabulary by projection on their difference, and split at the median. Split before scoring, with no dataset labels or image embeddings. This single partition does not guarantee lexical independence of all synonyms.

Primary test in the central-cross context. Give all arms the same observations. Compare (1) the six candidate interaction directions, (2) six additive regional predictions in that candidate, (3) six additive predictions of the query alone, (4) query directions plus candidate interaction directions, and (5) the same twelve-direction model with candidate additive directions replacing its interactions. Include a single matched interaction direction whose coefficient is inferred on calibration words, and the alternative photo in every pair. The query directions use only singleton and central observations in the primary context. Require the twelve-direction interaction model to improve predictive score on the true source over the query-only and equally sized additive-candidate alternative, and to improve its true-minus-foil margin over that equally sized control on both development splits. Bootstrap original source pairs. Test whether the discrimination persists on redraws; report secondary context separately. The method must still face category retrieval if these mechanism tests pass. No tuning of rank, temperature or fusion weights.
