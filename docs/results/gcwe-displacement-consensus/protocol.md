# Can one part of the sketch predict the displacement of correspondences in another part?

GCWE currently uses the correlation of pair distances. A preceding experiment showed much of its score can be associated with matching central to central and peripheral to peripheral patches. We test orientation-sensitive verification with the same native 7×7 CLIP correspondences. A quantized translation space is directly determined by grid offsets; every displacement is one of 13×13 possibilities. Translation is a restrictive hypothesis for category-level sketches, so a negative outcome is informative. The gallery nominees, word distributions, correction coefficient and all blank-canvas controls stay fixed.

Condition the frozen correspondence coupling separately on the even and odd checkerboards of sketch locations. For each half compute its histogram over the photo coordinate minus the sketch coordinate. Subtract the histogram formed by the product of the same source and photo location marginals. Let the two residual histograms be r0,r1. The donor statistic r0·r1 counts agreement of signed excess votes across the two parts. Either half having independent endpoints makes its contribution zero. In contrast, the average within-half squared norm rewards concentrated but mutually inconsistent votes. The gap between that control and the primary is exactly one half of the squared difference between the two residual histograms. No independence of the encoder activations across the two halves is assumed.

Repeat the calculation on the blank and subtract its score before applying the original GCWE word update. Controls use uncentered cross-histogram coincidence, within-half concentration with matched inputs, scrambled photo coordinates shared between both halves (preserving the underlying affinities), and the original distance verifier. Rank donors independently inside each opposite gallery fold. Geometric voting already has precedent in semantic correspondence, and spatial verification has been used in query expansion; the distinct empirical claim would concern *cross-part, marginal-corrected translation votes* producing better semantic feedback. Evaluate 1,536 queries from each Sketchy seed-42 permutation, positions 8,448–9,983. These queries are outside the earlier GCWE geometry decomposition pilot within the same split. Use the complete gallery and original KD evaluator, T4 reference and original regional readout. Promotion requires a gain in equal-split mean mAP over the original and positive mAP deltas over sham coordinates and within-half concentration on both splits. Record precision, paired class bootstrap uncertainty and no-GCWE results. Do not use TU for model selection.
