# Test partial semantic support against the same diffusion operator

The 16-photo support model cleared direct matching and arithmetic neighbor pooling, but it trailed the established RGB diffusion ranker. That contrast changed both the neighborhood and the statistical model. Here the normalized diffusion operator D from the existing single-view reference defines exactly the same candidate-specific empirical distribution for all readouts. Its data, bandwidth and step count are unchanged. The simple reference ranks by D[r], where r is the one-query likelihood ratio relative to its gallery-wide mean.

Under a partial semantic observation, a given photo can either express the aspect specified by the sketch or supply only background. Its likelihood ratio is 1−ρ+ρr. Estimate the log likelihood for a typical group of n photos by n·D[log(1−ρ+ρr)], conditional on the actual D-neighborhood. Integrate the exponentiated log likelihood over one uniform ρ shared by the group. This is an empirical *fractionally weighted likelihood*. For n equally weighted photos it gives the previous finite-group likelihood exactly. It is not the expectation over n independent random neighbor IDs: that alternative collapses to a monotone function of D[r], making new rankings impossible. The difference is sensitivity to the distribution of support, beyond its mean.

Use n=16 selected in the preceding same-family pilot. Record n=4 and 8 for sensitivity, a fixed half-support model, full-support expected log evidence, the locked arithmetic diffusion, and the direct semantic score. Approximate the integral in log space with 16 Gauss–Legendre nodes. Check against 32 nodes on the first batch of both splits by comparing centered score errors and exact evaluator AP. Report drift, including any metric ties crossed. No additive score on top of the previous framework, and no intervention on gallery examples. Test the same two seeded Sketchy pilot cohorts. Select by equal-split mean mAP among configurations improving ordinary diffusion on both splits, before examining further queries. Compare against the fixed-fraction and full-support readouts to determine whether fraction uncertainty itself earns credit. With positive held-back results, freeze before evaluating on all 30 TU-Berlin classes.

The operator follows Wang et al.'s image retrieval work and is an existing baseline component. The neighboring statistical precedents are the shared-membership likelihood in Bayesian Sets and the latent membership likelihood in MLESAC.
