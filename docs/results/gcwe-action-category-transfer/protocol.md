# Does a common geometric frame improve decisions about category on independent source photographs?

In the discovery assay the common-frame semantic risk favored the actual source against a different-category rival on both Sketchy splits. Either transfer across depictions or unusually close correspondence with the image used to draw the sketch could explain this. Freeze all operations, probabilities, gallery words, temperatures, Monte Carlo draws and the shared-frame formula from that assay. Do not use TU.

Take one seed-42 drawing per distinct source photo not appearing as a source or a foil in the discovery cohort of either split. Score the native symmetric whole-sketch public-word channel and its unchanged RGB diffusion for every such query against every official gallery photo. Use KD-SBIR for the reference mAP and precision. Form disjoint adjacent pairs among the native top 200 before inspecting relevance. Retain pairs with exactly one relevant class, excluding either endpoint if it is the sketch's own source photo or any discovery photo. To avoid heavily weighting ambiguous sketches, sample one eligible pair per query for each available direction (native decision correct/wrong) with a seed keyed to the query ID. The two candidate IDs supplied to the scorer are always in native rank order, not relevance order. Neither query label nor candidate label enters the operation forecast. The gallery and the retrieval backbone are the same as the official benchmark; selecting mixed pairs is a diagnostic evaluation on a subset.

Test whether the symmetric shared-frame semantic decision reaches above 50% balanced within-query accuracy and beats letting each candidate choose its own frame on both splits. Compute the 95% intervals across classes for paired differences and report how many queries have both native directions. Also measure the fixed native-coordinate control and the confidence of the candidate's geometric map. Report rescued versus damaged decisions, and mean Brier advantage of a relevant photo over a foil. All candidates are new relative to discovery; galleries and some classes can overlap across Sketchy splits. No official mAP is assigned to the label-selected diagnostic subset. Advancing to a deployable label-blind reranker or frozen TU requires passing the primary test on both splits. [Source-level intervention](../gcwe-shared-intervention-frame/protocol.md)
