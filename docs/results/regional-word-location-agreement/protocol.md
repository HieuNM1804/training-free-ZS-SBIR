# Can alternative public words agree on *where* a candidate photo is supported?

A gallery photo has a mixture b(w) of 64 public word identities. The baseline adds their regional evidence before choosing a region. Multiple visual-semantic interpretations of one image and local region–word retrieval already have precedent. The test here uses an exact inequality for the frozen mixture to measure the loss of agreement between its word-specific location profiles. [PIE-Net, CVPR 2019](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2019/html/Song_Polysemous_Visual-Semantic_Embedding_for_Cross-Modal_Retrieval_CVPR_2019_paper.html) · [SCAN, ECCV 2018](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_ECCV_2018/html/Kuang-Huei_Lee_Stacked_Cross_Attention_for_Image-Text_Matching_ECCV_2018_paper.html)

In one pose let e_rw denote the activation of word w in region r. Absorb the region prior and inverse gallery mean likelihood into a normalized regional measure ν. For L_r=sum b_w e_rw, the original regional likelihood is S=Eν[L²]/Eν[L]. If each latent word instead reads its own locations, the likelihood U=sum b_w Eν[e_w²]/Eν[e_w]. Let m_w=Eν[e_w], μ=Eν[L], λ_w=b_w m_w/μ and f_w=e_w/m_w. Then U−S is μ times the mean squared deviation of f_w from its λ-weighted mean in the ν measure. Hence U≥S; the discrepancy vanishes for words with proportional location profiles, even when their raw activations differ. For zero-activation words their zero contribution is defined by continuity. Average likelihoods across the two poses before taking logs. Check both the bound and an independent float64 reconstruction on actual gallery columns in every batch.

Primary uses log S + log(S/U), a one-factor penalty equal to the fraction of concentration energy retained by averaging the word location profiles. This choice is a hypothesis, not a consequence of the identity. Compare native log S, log U, the same penalty after collapsing position within each scale, and a sham that reassigns the exact penalty to a gallery photo at an adjacent native-score rank. External word sources, views and scoring coefficients remain fixed. No category labels or other sketches enter a ranking.

Fix seed 42, 3,072 queries per Sketchy split at positions 1,536 through 4,607, full galleries and original KD metrics. This sample is shared with the independent additive confirmation; this method is specified before seeing its results. Advance only if the global-plus-regional primary beats native on both splits and has positive mean mAP differences versus both the scale-only and near-score shams on both splits. Report the regional-only scores as a separate diagnostic. Subsequent validation must use a disjoint sample and locked TU transfer.
