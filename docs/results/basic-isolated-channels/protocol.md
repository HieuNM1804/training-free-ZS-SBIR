# Standalone channels before composition

Use frozen OpenAI CLIP ViT-B/32, one unmodified sketch and one unmodified gallery image per embedding. Compute three isolated scores: direct sketch–photo cosine, cosine between the photo and an inferred textual query, and overlap of the sketch and photo noun distributions. The second uses the source-prompt softmax over the 20,931 approved Open Images nouns to average the matching target-prompt embeddings, then normalizes the result. Only the first channel is entirely image-only. Both semantic alternatives infer words from the very same sketch, which makes correlated errors plausible. Keep the entire gallery in every method. No graph, regional hierarchy, contour, gradients, auxiliary encoder or pooled test queries participate.

Compare matched normalizations before comparing fusion rules. The main BASIC-style arm applies per-query gallery min-max normalization to visual and inferred-text scores and uses product minus 0.1 times squared sum. Mean and unpenalized product receive identical inputs. A second comparison uses empirical midranks for both channels and for the word-overlap alternative. Per-query gallery min-max and empirical ranks are explicit adaptations of BASIC's external-data min normalization. This is an isolated test of the fusion, not reproduction of the paper's complete system. The full-data rows and 0.1 penalty are fixed before running TU. Pilot-only single-component controls examine gallery feature centering, 250-dimensional noun PCA, a contrastive projection and top-five attention expansion. The contrastive matrices use across-noun variability of the two existing prompt banks' midpoints and the uncentered second moment of within-noun half-differences. This surrogate is distinct from the authors' generated object/style corpora. Gallery centering estimates a different reference mean from the authors' LAION mean. These controls do not enter the main fusion.

Record both official KD metrics and which sketches each standalone channel gets right on its own. Bound the benefit of selecting either *entire* ranking retrospectively. Measure top-200 overlap and whether the other channel correctly orders relevant versus irrelevant photos adjacent in the first channel's top 1,000. Use disjoint adjacent rank pairs and half credit for ties. These are post-ranking diagnostics only. Fixed full replications use Sketchy Ext-1/Ext-2 and the 30 unseen TU classes.
