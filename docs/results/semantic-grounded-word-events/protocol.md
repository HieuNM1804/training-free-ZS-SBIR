# Does a shared scene meaning also have the same local role in both depictions?

The candidate-specific deletion model improved some within-class predictions but could make a different-category foil more plausible as well. A global vocabulary match has a second observable implication: two words plausible in both images should have a compatible *relative assignment* to two positions. Test one joint event inside the global semantic retrieval module. Both global semantic identity and local placement are latent, and the same word identities must explain both. A product of separately computed visual and text scores cannot impose that constraint.

Draw one word independently from the intact sketch posterior a and the intact candidate posterior b. The probability of agreeing is G=Σ a_w b_w. Conditional on agreement the identity distribution is h_w=a_w b_w/G. Repeat this event twice, obtaining uncertain words u,v. Select an unordered pair of distinct corner positions uniformly. For each image, infer the assignment between these positions and u,v conditional on reporting the unordered multiset. Its binary probability π follows from the 2×2 odds ratio of the local word distributions. Independent assignments agree with probability [1+κ_q κ_d]/2, where κ=2π−1. Positive word-specific accessibility and region-specific exposure multipliers cancel from both conditional odds. These invariances describe frozen readouts, not a claim of actual image rotation invariance.

The matched event likelihood normalized per word is G sqrt(1+E_h⊗h,pairs[κ_q κ_d]). If placement supplies no information this reduces exactly to the native direct semantic score. Normalize before running the same RGB diffusion and KD evaluator. The model approximates global and local CLIP readings as separate conditional observations of the same word identities; their shared encoder makes that assumption imperfect. For tractability let each candidate expose local evidence for its 32 most probable global words; pairs involving another word have an unobserved assignment, integrated with probability 1/2. All 20,931 words remain in G. Report the matched mass included and a distribution-free bound 1−m² on the omitted correlation. This number quantifies the computational assumption and is not a proof of semantic grounding.

Use 384 sketch queries on each full Sketchy gallery, seed-42 permutation from offset 8,192 excluding every ID used in the three preceding geometry-to-word intervention cohorts in either split. Keep the native photo bank, split, graph, evaluator and backbone fixed. Assess:

* The matched-word joint event, native G alone and the joint event before and after RGB diffusion.
* Independent identity control: preserve both images' complete local readouts and the h weights, but draw independent word pairs for their assignments. Within each position pair the signed orientation has zero mean under iid words (antisymmetry). The expected independent correlation is identically zero. Include this analytical baseline, and also a nondegenerate control in which the *same* prior candidate words b, independent of the query's global identity, explain the local event.
* Random-frame averaging over all eight square symmetries exactly cancels the signed correlation since both assignments of every mapped pair are equiprobable. To avoid relying only on this degenerate null, use a fixed quarter-turn of candidate corner identities, and use the deterministically permuted attachment of its word names inside blocks of four native ranks. Report the average of the nonidentity quarter-turn and its inverse to avoid a preferred direction.
* Verify probabilities and marginalization by exhaustive enumeration on a small vocabulary, the antisymmetry null, invariance under random positive row/column rescaling, and that the correlation never exceeds m².

The semantic hypothesis requires a positive mean mAP effect across the two split-specific native scores and superiority over the independent-content and wrong-frame controls in mAP on each split; inspect precision and paired class uncertainty. Official category results and the effect on high-scoring competing classes are different endpoints. If credits go to generic localization while matched word identity carries no additional value, do not promote this explanation. Chosen fixed without TU.
