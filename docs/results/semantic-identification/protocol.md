# Which distinctions survive incomplete observation?

The global word channel uses one precise source posterior. Its order of semantic hypotheses and its confidence in them are different assumptions. The primary object of inference here is the set of gallery decisions identified by the observed ordering, with explicit abstention. An admissible vector represents a possible interpretation of the observed sketch. It is not a calibrated distribution of physical scene attributes.

For each native view v (the sketch and its horizontal reflection) sort all 20,931 source-prompt logits. Let U_vk be uniform over the first k words, admitting endpoints of equal-logit blocks only. Any nonincreasing probability vector respecting that order and its ties has the unique mixture weights λ_k=k(p_k−p_{k+1}) on these prefixes. Its expected word rank equals Σ λ_k(k+1)/2. For a pair of target photo posteriors b,c the associated utility is linear in λ, with coefficients y_k=mean_top-k(b−c). The lower and upper convex envelopes of the points ((k+1)/2,y_k) give sharp bounds at each permitted mean rank. Take extrema across the two view-specific sets, equivalent to their convex hull for linear objectives.

The primary model allows mean rank anywhere between those of the two native source distributions (each measured in its own ordering). The extremal distributions need not be either native view. This native concentration assumption is falsifiable. Report unrestricted monotone interpretations, the actual two native views alone, and the native overlap preference with exactly matched retained coverage. A certified decision means one candidate wins under every admissible interpretation to numerical tolerance. Check against independent small linear programs, bound inclusion of native scores, exact equality and reversal when the candidates swap. All word operations use frozen CLIP, no other sketch enters inference.

Use repeated sketches drawn from specific photographs in Sketchy. Independently in each class, pair the 100 original reference photos greedily by descending frozen visual cosine (ties by IDs). Select 12 disjoint pairs per class using seed 42, restricting reference photos to at least two drawings. Retain all individual sketches of each selected photo. As a category control, pair the same photo pool across different classes, greedily by visual cosine, dropping any unmatched photos. The pair construction is diagnostic evaluation; category and source identities never enter the semantic decision. The native category retrieval evaluator and official baselines are unchanged. Two same-class photos are both correct for category ZS-SBIR; the source photo is an additional instance-level diagnostic here.

For every sketch measure coverage and correct decisions among certified comparisons, same-source agreement with each other drawing scored independently, and the native and two-view controls. Use an equally sized native confidence subset selected by absolute two-candidate normalized margin; state that selection uses score confidence only and assess by class. The mechanistic hypothesis predicts accuracy and agreement exceeding the matched-confidence control on both same-class splits at nontrivial coverage. If the interval usually includes both directions or confident scalar margins are equally informative, the identification procedure does not earn a retrieval intervention. Both Sketchy splits are exploratory and overlap. No TU selection.

Set-valued decisions and dominance have precedent in evidential classification. The contribution under test is the joint use of sharp public-word rank constraints, per-sketch uncertainty and real redraws to expose distinctions that the global source channel can support across domains.
