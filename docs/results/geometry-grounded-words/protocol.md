# Does the nominee's word refer to the part that verified the sketch?

The full reference method gives each visual nominee one geometry score, then reads its words from its global CLIP photo vector. If recognizable background supplies a different noun, geometry can corroborate the correct shape while boosting an unrelated word. This pilot tests that failure at the original candidate level and on the unchanged complete gallery. It holds the ranked nominee weights fixed, varying only where the word reading comes from.

Let J(a,b) denote the frozen match distribution over query and nominee patches. With two draws under J, the native verifier computes the Pearson correlation of the distances between the selected query patches and the selected nominee patches. For a fixed match (a,b), integrate the product of centered distances over the second draw, using the common original standard deviations. This gives its contribution C(a,b). Summing J(a,b)C(a,b) yields the original correlation exactly. For each nominee location b subtract its baseline share J(b) times the overall correlation from its contribution. The resulting centered location weights sum to zero. Perform the same measurement on the uniform query and subtract the two location-weight vectors. Their weighted sum of native patch descriptors yields a query-dependent vector v. Its dot product with each frozen text direction measures how strongly spatial support coincides with that particular word's patch responses. Common shifts to every patch do not affect this quantity.

For each nominee tilt its original word posterior by exp(scale × v·text) and renormalize across all 20,931 nouns. Update the query as before, with the selected-to-proposal word frequency ratio, unchanged folds, eight draws and the fixed 0.65 term in the strong base. The prespecified primary changes the selected word reading while retaining the original proposal prior. Another ablation changes both. Reversing the local sign, reflecting patch identities or permuting them before scoring the nouns preserves the global nominee weights. Compare coefficient 1 with these matched controls at 1. A secondary factor of 4 has a same-strength shuffled control. Check whether actual word-to-position pairing improves both direct choice of a relevant nominee and the end retrieval result. Accuracy can improve in one but decline in the other. Benchmark labels enter the scorer's evaluation only. No gradient, crop selector or contour operation is involved.

Dense patch-to-text readout and spatial consistency have separate precedents. The part under test is the closed-form covariance between a noun's spatial response and the contribution to a correspondence with an individual query. [Zhou et al., ECCV 2022](https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2022/papers_ECCV/papers/136880687.pdf).
