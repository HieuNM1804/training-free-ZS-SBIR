# Is the way a particular sketch reports word content persistent across its parts?

The candidate-specific visibility calibration has a restricted interpretation. In log space its forecast is the sketch context mean plus the candidate's held-region deviation from its other regions. Its absolute word profile cancels. A geometric association can therefore improve ranking without recovering an actual word-omission mechanism. The pure calibrated forecast also predicted a withheld region worse than a context-only sketch on average. Test a falsifiable uncertainty model for when a candidate's region contrast transfers.

With one observed anchor region A, let model H0 predict p0(j)=q_A at any other region j. In model H1, p1(j) is proportional to q_A(w)b_j(w)/b_A(w). Here b describes the four independently encoded crops of a candidate photo; q_A is the separate CLIP readout from the sketch crop. Both hypotheses predict words from a common alphabet and give identical probabilities when the candidate has a flat semantic profile. They are exact respectively in the constant-content and shared positive word-reporting channel models. H1 cancels arbitrary word-specific multiplicative changes to the candidate and arbitrary scalar changes to each regional readout after normalization. This cannot identify an absolute probability that a human depicted a word.

Treat H as an unknown equal-prior binary scene hypothesis. For each of two additional observed regions B,C, the evidence is an agreement between one latent reported word from the model and one word from that region's frozen sketch readout. Under H this has probability p_H(j)·q_j. With independent word draws conditional on A,H, the two event likelihoods multiply. Bayes' rule gives an exact posterior on whether this candidate's profile transfers. Predict the fourth, previously unseen region by averaging the two forecasts under that posterior. Average over the three valid anchor choices for each withheld corner. Evidence only changes which explanation to use. The fourth region and independent redraws are never predictors. Check exact posterior and marginalization on a small hypothesis space and verify that perturbing a held-out sketch readout never changes its forecast.

Use every trial and stored photo split from the independent observation-channel study. Report expected log scores on (i) each drawing's own withheld readout and (ii) the corresponding corner of a different drawing made from the same source, averaging targets excluding the predictor sketch. Other drawings are accessed after forecasts are complete. Compare to H0, H1, a fixed mixture (independent H for each future corner with identical one-region marginals), and a shuffled explanation weight from the foil photo while all local word forecasts stay fixed. Include the strong previous context-only geometric-mean forecast using *all* visible regions. If persistent H improves the score against the fixed mixture but is below H0 or full context, apparent adaptation does not demonstrate useful candidate evidence. Require true-source gain over fixed mixture, H0 and swapped-weight controls on both splits for the redraws, and gain in discrimination versus foil over the fixed mixture. Also report same-class paired comparison accuracy. Confirm the effect is present across photo pairs rather than dominated by prolific redraw sources. This assay is mechanistic and post-selection, not official category mAP.
