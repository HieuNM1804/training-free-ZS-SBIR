# Confirm the additive regional estimator on held-apart queries

The originally proposed scale-plus-interaction score failed on the discovery sample. A secondary arm with both main effects and no interaction was near tied on Ext-1 and improved mAP by 0.261 points on Ext-2. The next evaluation is a new hypothesis derived from that secondary outcome. Use the frozen formula L10 + L01 − L00. In the original positive-evidence coordinates this is the product of the two main-effect likelihood ratios times the shared-attention score. Different candidate-dependent scale and position effects can therefore support the same candidate independently. The full global semantic term and both fusion weights are fixed.

Primary comparison is the original global-plus-regional score versus its additive counterpart without GCWE. Require an mAP improvement on each Sketchy split and a gain over each main effect alone (scale-only and position-only) on each split. Also report regional-only AP and precision. Lock seed 42, permutation positions 1,536 through 4,607 on each split (3,072 queries), entire native gallery, and the source KD evaluator. No query from discovery is reused in confirmation within its own split. Sketchy was used in other historical experiments, and the two splits overlap. T4 replay of the original score uses its own 16-query batches and the original expression before algebraic simplification. Transfer to full evaluation with GCWE and TU requires confirmation. No parameters are selected on TU.
